Patterns

The rules for combining components on a screen.

  • Page frame Every page is the top bar, then main with 20px padding (16px on a phone) and 20px between blocks.
  • Panel head The h1 with its sub on the left, at most one primary action on the right.
  • Notices Skipped files: the notice goes under the panel head, above the table, and the numbers still show.
  • Shading Cells shade in five solid blue steps, relative to the largest compared cell: step = max(1, ceil(5 × value ÷ largest)).
  • Empty and not-found states With no data, a page keeps its frame and its panel head, and says so in one line: in the table for the overview, under the head for Craft versions.
  • Number formats Numbers are mono and right aligned.
