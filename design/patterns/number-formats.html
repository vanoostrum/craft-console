Number formats

Numbers are mono and right aligned. Cost: €1,234.50, with " *" when a model has no price. Time: 27m under an hour, 5h02m under 100 hours, 196h from 100 hours. No data: "—". An estimate starts with "≈". A change: "▼ 20% vs 2.8.2" in green, "▲ 26% vs 2.7.0" in red, "0% vs 2.8.1" plain.
